## English Version

### 1. Introduction
Welcome to **Tutor Connect**. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data of our users, including tutors, guardians, and students. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (**Tutor Connect**).

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using the app, you consent to the data practices described in this policy.

---

### 2. Information We Collect
We collect information that identifies, relates to, or describes you ("Personal Information"). The types of data we collect include:

#### A. Personal & Account Information
- **Registration Details:** Name, email address, phone number, password, and account role (Tutor or Guardian).
- **Authentication Data:** Firebase Authentication credentials and Google Sign-In profile info.

#### B. Profile & Verification Information (Tutors & Guardians)
- **Tutor Profiles:** Educational background, university/department, subject expertise, tuition preferences, profile photos, and salary expectations.
- **Verification Documents:** National ID (NID), student ID card, or certificate images provided by tutors strictly for identity verification.
- **Guardian Posts:** Tuition requirements, class level, subject details, location area, and budget.

#### C. Location Information
- Approximate location (City, Area, District) provided by users or autofilled to display nearby tuition posts and available tutors.

#### D. Messages & Support Content
- Messages and image attachments sent through our private **Helpline Chat** feature for customer support and dispute resolution.

#### E. Device & Usage Information
- Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) push notification tokens, device model, operating system version, and anonymous crash/analytics logs.

---

### 3. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected information for the following purposes:
- **Core App Functionality:** Facilitate matching between verified tutors and guardians.
- **Identity Verification:** Authenticate tutor profiles to ensure platform safety and prevent fraud.
- **Privacy-First Communication:** Mediate contacts securely without exposing private phone numbers or exact residential addresses publicly on the platform.
- **Support & Customer Care:** Respond to inquiries via private Helpline chat and resolve issues.
- **Notifications:** Send timely updates regarding tuition applications, status approvals, and messages.

---

### 4. Privacy-First Commitment & Contact Protection
- **No Public Contact Exposure:** Personal phone numbers and exact home addresses are **never** displayed publicly on open tutor directories or tuition post listings.
- **Strict Verification Storage:** Identity verification documents (NIDs/Student IDs) are accessible solely by authorized administrators for compliance and verification purposes. They are never shared with third parties or other users.

---

### 5. Third-Party Services
We utilize trusted third-party service providers to power key features of Tutor Connect:
- **Firebase Authentication & Cloud Firestore:** For secure sign-in, database management, and rule-based authorization.
- **Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM):** For delivering real-time push notifications.
- **Google Sign-In Services:** For convenient user authentication.

These third parties process your data strictly in accordance with their respective privacy policies and security standards.

---

### 6. Data Security
We implement robust technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information:
- All data in transit is encrypted using industry-standard **TLS / HTTPS**.
- Database access is restricted via strict **Firestore Security Rules** and Firebase Custom Claims.

---

### 7. Data Retention and Account Deletion
- **Retention:** We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to provide platform services and comply with legal obligations.
- **Account Deletion:** Users have the right to request deletion of their account and associated data at any time.
  - **In-App Deletion:** Navigate to **Profile → Settings → Account Deletion** within the Tutor Connect app.
  - **Web Deletion Request:** Users can also request account deletion online by emailing `support@tutorconnect.com` with their registered email address.
  - Upon account deletion, profile data, active tuition listings, and verification files will be permanently deleted or anonymized from our active databases.

---

### 8. Children's Privacy
Tutor Connect is intended for adult tutors (university students/graduates) and guardians. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.

---

### 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within the app or on our documentation pages with an updated "Last Updated" date.
