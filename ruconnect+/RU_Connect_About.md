# About RU Connect

**RU Connect** is an all-in-one digital platform developed for the students, teachers, alumni, staff, and communities of the **University of Rajshahi**. The app brings important university information, campus services, and useful resources together in one convenient place.

## Key Features

- University notices, news, and updates
- Student communities and organizations
- RU contact directory
- Alumni information
- Important university links
- Student insurance information
- Medical and emergency support
- Job library and job circulars
- Blood donor information
- University bus schedule
- RU website access
- RUCSU information
- Student helpline

## Our Mission

Our mission is to make university life easier by providing reliable, organized, and easily accessible campus information through a modern mobile application.
